How Privacy Grades Work

Grades are assigned based on how respectfully a service treats your personal data, rights, and privacy.

GRADE A

Very Safe

The terms are fair, transparent, and user-friendly. No tracking across other apps, no data selling, and you keep full ownership of your content.

GRADE B

Good & Fair

The service treats your data reasonably. It collects minimal necessary diagnostics and gives fair notice before modifying its terms.

GRADE C

Average / Standard

Typical commercial agreements (e.g. streaming, gaming). Standard diagnostics and marketing cookies, but generally predictable.

GRADE D

Concerning

Contains questionable clauses: forced arbitration (giving up your right to sue in court), broad behavioral tracking, or terms changes without warning.

GRADE E

High Risk

Significant red flags: sells your personal info to third-party data brokers, tracks you across other apps, or claims permanent rights to your uploaded work.
